Common questions

Do I need agi pipe behind a retaining wall?
In most cases, yes. Water building up behind a wall adds pressure and is a common cause of walls leaning or failing. Agi pipe run along the base of the wall and wrapped in drainage gravel gives that water somewhere to go. See our retaining wall supplies guide.
Why do you only sell socked agi pipe?
The sock stops fine soil washing into the slots and clogging the pipe. Unsocked pipe is cheaper up front but tends to silt up, so we only stock socked.
Do you deliver concrete sleepers?
Yes. Sleepers go out on our crane truck and can be placed where you need them on site, subject to access. We deliver across the Surf Coast, Geelong and the Bellarine. See our delivery areas.
