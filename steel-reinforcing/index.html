Reinforcing mesh and steel in Torquay

We carry concrete reinforcing mesh, deformed bar and the accessories that go with them for builders and concretors across Torquay, Geelong and the Surf Coast. Everything is in stock at our Boneyards Avenue yard and can go straight to site on our crane truck, so there's no need to wrestle 6m sheets onto a trailer.

Concrete mesh: SL72, SL82 and SL92

Our slab mesh comes in standard 6m x 2.4m sheets. The number after "SL" relates to the bar size: the higher the number, the heavier the mesh.

  • SL72 is commonly used for paths, small pads and lighter-duty slabs.
  • SL82 is the everyday choice for residential driveways, house slabs and garage floors.
  • SL92 is heavier again, for slabs where the engineer specifies more steel.

Your engineer's drawings always take priority. Bring them in and we'll match the mesh, trench mesh and bar to the spec.

Common questions

What mesh do I need for a driveway?
SL82 is the most common mesh for residential driveways around Torquay and Geelong, but always follow your engineer's or council's specification. Our slab and driveway guide covers everything else you'll need for the pour.
How many mesh sheets do I need?
Each 6m x 2.4m sheet is 14.4m², but sheets have to overlap. As a rough guide, allow about 12.5m² of coverage per sheet once lapped, then round up. Call us with your slab dimensions and we'll check the numbers.
Can you cut reinforcing bar to length?
Yes. We offer a cut-to-length service in-store for N12, N16 and N20 bar.
Do you deliver mesh to site?
Yes. Mesh sheets and bundles of steel go out on our crane truck across the Surf Coast, Geelong and the Bellarine. See our delivery areas.
