Delivery Areas

Crane truck delivery within about 50km of our Torquay yard, from Lorne to Lara and across the Bellarine.

Building supplies delivered across the Surf Coast and Geelong

Torquay Building Supplies delivers from our yard at 4 Boneyards Avenue to building sites, farms and backyards within about 50km. That covers the Surf Coast and Great Ocean Road to Lorne, the Armstrong Creek growth area, all of Geelong, the Bellarine Peninsula and the hinterland out to Winchelsea, Inverleigh and Bannockburn.

Our signwritten crane truck handles the heavy, awkward stuff: concrete sleepers, mesh and reinforcing steel, pavers and drainage, and palletised concrete bags. It can lift over fences and place loads where you need them, subject to site access.

Booking a delivery

Call (03) 5264 7963 or send us a quote request with your suburb, what you need and when. Let us know about tight access, overhead power lines or soft ground so we send the truck prepared.

Common questions

How far do you deliver?
We deliver across roughly a 50km radius of our Torquay yard: the Surf Coast out to Lorne, Winchelsea and Birregurra, all of Geelong out to Lara, the Bellarine Peninsula, and the Golden Plains townships around Bannockburn. If you're just outside the area, call and ask.
What can you deliver?
Anything too big for the ute: concrete sleepers, mesh sheets, reinforcing steel, pavers, bagged concrete by the pallet, drainage pipe and bulk orders. Our crane truck can lift loads onto site, subject to access.
How much does delivery cost?
It depends on the load and the distance from Torquay. Call (03) 5264 7963 with your suburb and order and we'll quote it.
Can I pick up from the yard instead?
Yes. We're at 4 Boneyards Avenue, Torquay, open Monday to Friday 7:00am to 4:30pm and Saturday 8:00am to 12:00pm.
