Common questions

Which artificial turf pile height should I choose?
For kids and pets, a 30mm or 35mm pile is hard-wearing and easy to keep upright. For a front lawn that's mostly for looks, a 40mm pile feels fuller underfoot. We have samples in store.
Will corten steel stain my pavers?
While corten develops its rust patina, run-off can mark light-coloured pavers and concrete. It settles down once the patina forms, so plan edging placement with that in mind.
Can you deliver turf and pavers?
Yes. We deliver across the Surf Coast, Geelong and the Bellarine. See our delivery areas or call (03) 5264 7963.
